# Diez Hook Skill — the opening lines, up to the player's name

Built from all 142 written scripts in Diez's Notion FOOTBALL database (every Posted, Done, Recording and Sent to Editor page, May–October 2026). "The hook" means everything spoken before the story proper starts — usually the lines before the player's full name first appears ("Antonio Nusa came from Oslo…"). Spelling in the examples is lightly corrected; the wording is Diez's.

---

## The core formula (about 3 in 4 player stories)

Three short lines, then the name.

1. **"Imagine…" — put the viewer inside the wound or the peak, in second person.** One breath, 6–14 words. The lowest point ("Imagine losing your father at just two years old") or the highest ("Imagine being the best midfielder in the world").
2. **"You…" — escalate with one concrete, specific detail.** An age, a number, a fee, a ban length, a betrayal. Still second person. This line makes line 1 real.
3. **The turn — starts with "But", "And", "And years later", "But then", "But one day".** The inversion: the fall after the peak, the rise after the wound, or the secret behind the public moment. Often shifts to third person ("that same boy", "that same man", "the boy who…").

Then: **"[Full name] came from [place], a [working-class / forgotten / poor] corner of [city/country]…"** — the first time the name is spoken.

Two-line hooks work when line 2 carries both escalation and turn (Nusa, Max Dowman, Calafiori, Vardy, Figo, Ferran leaving).

---

## The rules

- **The name stays out of the hook.** The viewer is hooked by the life before they know whose it is. Exceptions are deliberate: the ceremonial full name as the hook's punchline ("But you can never count out, Lionel Andrés Messi Cuccittini"), or a direct address ("But Mario, maybe the question was not why always you…").
- **Second person in lines 1–2.** "Imagine…", "You…", "Your own…". The viewer lives it before they watch it.
- **One specific number in the first two lines whenever the story has one.** "at sixteen", "347 days later", "16 years in prison", "a 4-year ban", "18 months later", "fourteen years later", "10 days later", "two red cards", "five buses", "120 million euros", "twenty-five people under one roof".
- **Line 3 is a time-jump or a reversal.** "years later", "decades later", "ten years later", "but one day", "but then", "and almost overnight".
- **Superlatives set the stakes in four words.** "the best left back on the planet", "the biggest talent in your country's history", "the fastest man in football", "the most hated man in football", "the most promising midfielder of your generation".
- **Family, grief and betrayal hit hardest.** Fathers (absent, jailed, kidnapped, a legend, dying), mothers who sacrificed, a sister poisoned, a brother who stepped aside, parents who abandoned, a girlfriend who left, a best friend whose heart stopped.
- **Recurring turn lines Diez reaches for** (use the idea, vary the words): "But there is nothing more dangerous than a [team / nation / man] with nothing left to lose." · "But the only person you want to tell will never be there to listen." · "…football reaches down and takes it all away." · "…what could have been."
- **Never:** a question as the very first line of a story, "Did you know", a stat before the human detail, hype words, a generic "this is the story of…".

## Story-hook archetypes — every real example

### 1. Wound → glory (the most common)
Open at the lowest moment, turn to the triumph.
- Imagine being two years old, fighting for your life in a hospital bed. / Your own mother and father abandon you and only come back asking for millions when they see your success. / But Mario, maybe the question was not why always you, but how could it have been anyone else. *(Balotelli)*
- Imagine at sixteen, doctors tell you you may never be able to run again. / The world said your career was over, but 347 days later you come back and turn into one of the best defenders in the world. *(Calafiori)*
- Imagine your father abandoning you at birth. / You are born into the most dangerous country on the planet and are so poor you play football with a ball made of paper. / And on a single June night, a whole bleeding country went silent waiting on the boy who was never meant to survive. *(Gonzalo Plata)*
- Imagine a boy sleeping in a refugee hotel. / You hear bombs dropping outside that have already buried your grandfather. / But years later, he would carry a nation smaller than most cities to the edge of glory. *(Modrić)*
- Imagine a ten-year-old boy in the poorest part of Argentina, too small for his age. / Doctors tell him he will not ever grow and his dream is shattered before it becomes real. / But one day, that same boy will be the one to lift the whole world into the sky. *(Messi retires)*
- Imagine the moment you crack your skull against another man. / You lie motionless on the grass of a silent stadium that thinks it has just witnessed the end. / And six years later, you rise from the dead to score in the opening match of a World Cup in front of your country. *(Raúl Jiménez)*
- Imagine being twenty-five, stacking shelves in a factory, dreaming of professional football. / Every scout tells you you're too old to chase that dream, but that same man will go on to break a Premier League record no one thought could ever fall. *(Vardy)*
- Imagine at 15 you cross an ocean chasing a dream, alone in a country that speaks a language you do not know. / You suffer racist abuse from the moment you land, but you are so good at football that even the haters will learn to love everything about you. *(Okocha)*
- Imagine being rejected by your own nation. / The only friends you ever had come to you begging for money, but years later you will become one of the biggest talents in your country's history. *(Nusa)*
- Imagine getting two red cards in the most important games of your life. / You then take two months off to recover because your own fans don't want to see your face. / The following year you go on loan to Liverpool and become one of the best defenders on the planet. *(Araújo)*
- Imagine being a boy in Colombia, kicking a ball against a house that isn't yours. / You cross a border with nothing but your talent and a hunger no map can measure. / And decades later, a country that once questioned your name weeps it as their own. *(Julián Quiñones)*
- Imagine getting hit by a bullet. / You are rushed to hospital and beg the doctors not to let you die. / And 10 years later, you become the heartbeat of your nation on the biggest stage football has ever built. *(Gustavo Puerta)*
- Imagine being born in a village of two hundred souls. / There is no pitch, no school, no reason for the world to ever learn your name. / And that same boy will stand in the middle of heartbreak and a country's dream. *(Cubarsí)*
- Imagine your parents sleeping in a van at the edge of a football pitch. / The world tells you that you were never meant to make it. / But one night, you score the goal that puts your country in a World Cup final. *(Pedro Porro)*
- Imagine being born in a city so small the maps don't bother naming it. / Every day you take five buses to reach the nearest training ground, in search of a dream. / And at 34, you have the chance to carry your country to the top of the world. *(Salah)*
- Imagine being born in a refugee camp. / Your parents fled Liberia with nothing but each other and a baby the world was not supposed to remember. / And twenty-five years later, that same child leads his country into the first knockout phase in its history. *(Alphonso Davies)*
- Imagine watching your cousin bleed to death with your own eyes. / You are born into the most dangerous country there has ever been for a young black man. / And the only thing between you and that darkness is a leather ball, and a space you swear to protect with your life. *(Ronwen Williams)*
- Imagine your father abandoning you when you were two years old. / Growing up with your mother in poverty, leaving home at fourteen to travel 1,400 kilometres in search of a dream. / And then, eight years after that bus ride, scoring the most beautiful goal of the twenty-first century in a World Cup match. *(James Rodríguez)*
- Imagine being sacked from the only club you ever loved. / Your name is tarnished and your people will never look at you the same. / But decades later, the same man they laughed at will make his country champion of the world. *(Xavi)*
- Imagine winning the Ballon d'Or and standing on top of the world. / Then the worst injury in football tears it all away, and everyone counts you out. / But the man they buried would carry a country out of its grave. *(Rodri)*
- Imagine the biggest chance in a World Cup final. / A whole nation turns against you as the man who can never finish. / And then, one night, you become the man who ends sixteen years of waiting. *(Ferran Torres)*

### 2. Glory → fall
Open at the summit, turn to what took it away.
- Imagine being the most promising midfielder of your generation. / You become world champion at 25 with your country at your feet. / But a doping test and the betrayal of your own brother are waiting in the shadows to steal everything you have. *(Pogba)*
- Imagine being the fastest man in football. / One of the Premier League's most expensive players, and you get hit with a 4-year ban. / 18 months later it gets lifted and you can play again, but that boy is already dead inside. *(Mudryk)*
- Imagine being the biggest talent in your country's history. / European and English champion at 21 and you are on top of the world. / And years later, that gift becomes the very thing the whole world uses to break you. *(Foden)*
- Imagine being called the next promise, and then sold by your own club as a failure. / You then come back as one of the most exciting talents in the Premier League, but in the very moment you stand on top of the world, football reaches down and takes it all away. *(Brobbey)*
- Imagine losing your father at just two years old. / You come up as one of the best wingers in English history, but years later the world can only ask itself what could have been. *(Sterling)*
- Imagine walking into a broken Barcelona going into its ruin. / You score a hat-trick in El Clásico and win the hearts of the whole world. / And then they tell you they can't afford to keep the man who saved them. *(Aubameyang)*
- Imagine lining up for the World Cup with the best generation in your country's history. / You are the most promising defender of the tournament and have just signed your European dream with AC Milan. / But 10 days later, the same hands that applaud you will be the ones taking your life. *(Andrés Escobar)*
- Imagine being the most gifted boy your country has produced in a generation. / You play with Messi at Barcelona and you are on top of the world. / But years on, you will just be known as the prince who never became king. *(Neymar)*
- Imagine being the best number 10 on the planet. / You have the best season of your life and with a World Cup on the horizon, you dare to dream with everything. / And in the very last game before the World Cup, your foot breaks and your dream is shattered. *(Fermín López)*
- Imagine you start the World Cup as the most promising midfielder of the tournament. / You win the opening game for your country as man of the match. / And then, on the very night you reach the summit, football cuts the rope. *(Ismaël Koné)*
- Imagine you break your knee in the middle of the World Cup. / You have surgery and recovery seems almost impossible. / But 25 days later, football breaks your heart on the biggest stage in history. *(Baresi)*
- Imagine you become champion of Europe at 21 and you are on top of the world. / The most promising winger on the continent, until an injury breaks you into pieces. / And the man his country had already forgotten will one day assist a goal into history. *(Nico Williams)*
- Imagine winning the Champions League twice by 21. / You score the only goal for your nation in your first ever World Cup. / And instead of having you on their shoulders, your own country turns against you. *(João Neves)*
- Imagine being the best teenager on the planet. / You play with Messi at Barcelona and the world thinks you are next to the throne. / And from that day, you carry a name that will outrun you for the rest of your life. *(Bojan)*
- Imagine being the best midfielder of your generation, / playing one last World Cup with your country, with the chance to retire as world champion in your last ever game. / And then knocking down everything you had built across 17 years and killing a whole nation's dream. *(Zidane)*
- Imagine winning 5 NBA championships. / You are the best player to have ever lived and at 37 you retire to give time back to your family. / But one January morning, a helicopter climbs into the fog, and you and your daughter will never come back. *(Kobe)*

### 3. Hidden pain behind the public moment
The world sees one thing; the truth is the opposite.
- Imagine crying on the grass after your country is knocked out. / The cameras think you are broken because you lost. / But you're weeping because the man they tried to take from you is finally there to watch. *(Luis Díaz)*
- Imagine you carry a coffin's weight in total silence. / You get your dream at a World Cup and for ninety minutes a whole nation screams your name. / But not one of them knows that your heart is already broken. *(Jayden Adams)*
- Imagine your father gets 16 years in prison for planning a murder. / You grow up without ever knowing his voice, and years later you score twice against the club that saved your life. / But you cannot bring yourself to celebrate a single one. *(João Pedro)*
- Imagine scoring the most important goal of your life. / You fall to the grass because you cannot hold the pain you are bearing. / And the only person you ever wanted to see will never open his eyes. *(Gakpo)*
- Imagine playing your first professional game against Real Madrid. / Two years later, that very club pays 120 million euros to make you their own. / But the only person you want to tell will never be there to listen. *(Yan Diomandé)*
- Imagine being the most hated man in football. / The world makes fun of your long hair, but it doesn't know the only reason you wear it / is so your own son can recognise his father. *(Cucurella)*
- Imagine being the fastest man in football. / You are European champion at just 20 years old and you are on top of the world. / But the only thing you could never outrun is your own mind. *(Alphonso Davies)*
- Imagine writing the most important goal in your country's history. / You lift your shirt to the sky — and underneath, there is a message for a man who will never see it. *(Iniesta)*

### 4. Love, loyalty and the choice
Money vs home, a club vs values, a person who left.
- Imagine being the best midfielder in the world. / You carry your country to its second World Cup and everyone says you will join Real Madrid. / But then, that same man would choose a club's values over money. *(Rodri)*
- Imagine being the best left back on the planet. / You win it all, and then they ship you to a soulless league to be forgotten. / And drowning in millions, you would burn every one of them just to go home. *(Cancelo)*
- Imagine at fourteen you are photographed painting the streets in poverty while your country hosts a World Cup. / Your own girlfriend dumps you because you are too poor, and when you finally make it, every goal you score will be for her. *(Gabriel Jesus)*
- Imagine growing up loving the colours of only one badge. / You become the top scorer of the academy, and the same season your dream is meant to come true, your own mother takes you away. *(Pablo García)*
- Imagine your biggest dream is playing for Barcelona. / You sign for Atlético Madrid and 2 years later the club of your dreams offers 100 million euros for you. / And without knowing it, Madrid would turn into a golden prison. *(Julián Álvarez)*
- Imagine you are the captain of the club you swore to love forever. / You kiss the badge every time you score, but then you sign for your biggest rival, and in a matter of months you go from loved to the most hated player in the world. *(Figo)*
- Imagine a club drowning in debt, spending money it doesn't have to save you. / They carry you through every miss, and on the night you finally fly, you turn and throw away the light that guided you in the dark. *(Ferran leaving)*
- Imagine being so bad the world only knows you for a meme. / A wasted German starboy who was too big for his own good. / And the same forgotten boy has signed for the only club he ever dreamed of. *(Adeyemi)*

### 5. Fathers, families and inheritance
- Imagine being born in a city where your father is already a god before you can walk. / You inherit his name before it is ever your own. / And thirty years later, you answer him in the last minute of the game in front of the whole world. *(Mikel Merino)*
- Imagine your parents crossing the Sahara on foot just so you could exist. / Everything you built collapsing in a single injured second. / And after a year that tried to bury you, you give the glory away. *(Nico Williams)*
- Imagine watching your father weep into his hands. / You are three years old, and you swear to him that one day you will make it right. / And the day fate finally gives you that chance, it breaks you forever. *(Baggio)*
- Imagine being the best football player to ever play the game. / 4 Champions Leagues, 8 Ballon d'Ors, one World Cup — but none of it matters, because your guiding light has left you forever. *(Jorge Messi)*
- Imagine lifting the biggest trophy in club football, and the smallest hand in the stadium is the one that makes it real. / Your daughter runs to the centre circle to plant a flag in the grass. / A few years later you win the same trophy again, but this time she is no longer by your side. *(Luis Enrique / Xana)*
- Imagine leaving your family asleep in bed while you go to work. / You are hours away, chasing a football match, when the ground back home tears itself apart. / And by the time you claw through the concrete with your own hands, the three people you love most are already gone. *(Lucas Trejo)*
- Imagine being a child and your older brother never coming home. / He was sent to a cold island in the South Atlantic to fight a war he didn't choose. / And four years later, in a football match, you will avenge every mother who lost her son. *(Maradona / Malvinas)*
- Imagine you are 20 years old and your heart suddenly stops in the middle of a football match. / You lie motionless in a stadium that knows it has just witnessed the end. / But your best friend will pull your shirt over his shoulders — and make you immortal in front of the whole world. *(Saibari / Nouri)*

### 6. The prodigy warning
For teenagers — the hook is the danger of the hype.
- Imagine being the best teenager in English football. / You play your first Premier League game at 16, but the same spotlight that crowns you is the one that has buried a hundred stars before. *(Max Dowman)*
- Imagine being the biggest talent in world football. / At just 15 years old all the European greats want to sign you, but sometimes we forget football has buried more prodigies than it has crowned. *(JJ Gabriel)*
- Imagine six months ago you were just a kid in Egypt with eight first-team games to your name. / You've never scored in your own league, never worn your senior country's shirt. / And almost overnight the biggest club in the world calls you the heir to the throne. *(Hamza Abdelkarim)*
- Imagine a photograph taken in 2008. / A one-year-old boy, bathed by the hands of Lionel Messi himself. / And nobody in that frame knows they are staring at the future of football. *(Lamine Yamal)*
- Imagine your biggest hero retiring from international football. / That same day you play in your home stadium and score the goal we have seen 1,000 times before. *(Lamine / Messi)*

### 7. Nations and underdog teams
- Imagine a country of four million people, born from a war that turned its towns to rubble and its children into refugees. / And twenty-seven years later, it stood one game from ruling the world. *(Croatia 2018)*
- Imagine being a country that has never won a game at a major tournament. / You face Ronaldo in the opening game in front of his people. / But there is nothing more dangerous than a team of men from a country with nothing left to lose. *(Greece 2004)*
- Imagine you are one of the smallest nations ever to walk onto a World Cup. / Ten islands in the middle of the Atlantic with half a million people, and across from you stand the champions of Europe. / But there is nothing more dangerous than a small nation chasing a dream it was never supposed to have. *(Cape Verde)*
- Imagine watching your hometown club drown in its misery. / You go from playing against Manchester City to the fourth tier of Spanish football. / And then, a generation of dreamers refuse to watch it die, and raise it from its grave. *(Racing Santander)*
- Imagine surviving just seven points clear of relegation. / That same summer you dare to dream of winning the Premier League. / And when you live long enough to become champions of England, the sky takes it all away. *(Leicester / Vichai)*
- Imagine spending your whole life being told you were born too small. / No academy, no fame, and a day job waiting for you every Monday morning. / But one night, you take everything the giants have and you break it in two. *(Norway 1981)*
- **Group-of-death template:** "The World Cup has its group of death: [giant], [giant], [giant]. These world champion nations will fight it out… Oh, and [minnow] is the fourth nation in the group." *(Costa Rica, Cape Verde)*

### 8. The aphorism opener (retirements, endings, legends)
- They say an athlete dies twice — once at the end of life, and once the day he retires. *(Ronaldo retiring; Messi retirement)*
- They say athletes die twice, once at the end of life, and once when they retire. / But imagine the day your own heart forces you to die that second death, years before you were ever ready. *(Agüero's heart)*
- A winner is just a loser who kept on trying. *(What if Messi had given up)*
- When destiny is already in your hands, you don't look up at the coin. *(Maradona's coin toss)*
- Believe me, eyes don't know how to keep secrets. *(Messi's eyes)*

### 9. The last-minute cold open (a single moment)
Drop straight into the decisive second, before any backstory.
- It's the last minute of the game. / Otamendi fails to clear and France are through on goal. / And suddenly one man stands between a nation's dream and 36 years of heartbreak. *(Dibu Martínez)*
- It's the last minute of the game. / Canada push forward with one final chance to make history. / And the ball falls to a man who buried both his parents, and he makes sure the whole world will still remember their names. *(Eustáquio)*
- It's the last minute of the game. / Ireland are seconds away from beating France and qualifying for the World Cup. / But the greatest legend in French football was about to make sure they never arrive. *(Henry's handball)*
- It's the last minute of the game. / Mexico are beating Holland and they are ninety seconds away from the quarter-final. / And since that day, three words are carved into the heart of every Mexican. *(No era penal)*
- Imagine the 116th minute of a World Cup final. / Ninety years of waiting on the shoulders of a man who is playing for the best friend he buried with his own hands. *(Iniesta)*
- Imagine scoring a goal in the last minute of a World Cup semi-final, in your own country. / But when you raise your eyes and see the scoreboard, you realise you're receiving the greatest humiliation in the history of sport. *(Mineirazo)*
- Imagine being one goal away from winning your country its first ever World Cup. / Having the best midfield the world has ever seen. / And in the 62nd minute, a whole country's hopes lay on your shoulders. *(Casillas)*

### 10. The news peg (something happening now)
Open on today's news, then pull the past into it.
- Imagine scoring the most famous goal in Premier League history. / You give your club its first title in 50 years, but fourteen years later, being told it might count for nothing. *(Agüero 93:20 — the 115 charges)*
- Portugal are through to the round of 16. / Eleven men chasing the dream of a first star above its badge. / Some will do it for Ronaldo, but others will do it for him. *(Diogo Jota)*
- Uruguay have lost to Spain and are eliminated. / The two-time world champions are the biggest disappointment of the tournament. / But the world forgets the summer where three million people stood at the gates of glory. *(Uruguay 2010)*
- Senegal have qualified for the World Cup for the very first time. / They play the reigning world champions in the opening game, in what should be an easy win for the French. / But football doesn't care who you are. It only asks you to believe when no one else does. *(Senegal 2002)*
- Argentina are the reigning champions of the world. / Their captain is injured and may not make the opening game. / But you can never count out Lionel Andrés Messi Cuccittini. *(Messi hat-trick)*
- Imagine dragging your country to its third final in three years. / You miss the penalty in the biggest game of your life and in tears you tell a whole nation you are done forever. / But ten years later, you walk out of the very same tunnel for the last game you will ever play. *(Messi at MetLife)*
- Imagine being thirty-nine years old, the whole world telling you the throne already belongs to someone else. / And on the very day you were born, you answer them in the only language they cannot argue with. *(Messi's birthday)*
- 24 hours until the first World Cup of Lamine Yamal, of Erling Haaland, of Michael Olise. / But while some start to write their story, others will have one last dance. *(24 hours)*

## Take, explainer and pattern videos (not life stories)

When the request is an opinion, an analysis or a "did you notice" — not a life story — Diez opens with a bold claim, never "Imagine":
- **"[X] has a HUGE problem that nobody can see / nobody wants to say."** (Spain, England, Barcelona, the World Cup hydration breaks)
- **"How is nobody talking about…"** (Pedri's prediction, the World Cup ball, France's "rigged" path)
- **"This CANNOT be a coincidence."** / **"Please tell me you noticed."**
- **"Why is every single player at the World Cup wearing pink cleats?"** — the one place a question opens.
- **"There are only three certain things in life: death, taxes, and Spain beating France."**
- **"We all know that [team] won the [year] World Cup. But how, exactly, did they do it?"** (series format)
- **"They said he was finished."** / **"They told you it was over."** — anaphora of the doubters, then the answer.
- **Anaphora list:** "Bernardo Silva is not Messi, and he was not sent off. Hakimi is not Messi…"
- **Direct dare:** "I dare you to hear this story and not cry."
- **Hot take:** "There is only one person who can stop Lamine Yamal from winning the Ballon d'Or this year, and that is Ferran Torres."

## How to write 5 options for a new player

Give five genuinely different hooks, not five rewordings:
1. **Wound → glory** from his lowest documented moment.
2. **Glory → fall** (or fall → glory) from his highest moment.
3. **Hidden pain** — the thing the public didn't know behind a public moment.
4. **A person** — the father, mother, sibling, partner, friend or mentor at the heart of it.
5. **Wildcard** — whichever fits best: the last-minute cold open, the news peg, the prodigy warning, the choice, the nation/underdog, or the aphorism.

Every fact in a hook must be true and come from the research — a wrong age or year in line 1 kills the video. Match the archetype to the story; don't force a comeback onto a tragedy.
